How to choose

  1. Videos from data, templates, or AI agents. HyperFrames if you think in HTML/CSS. Remotion if you are a React team and fine with its license. Motion Canvas or Revideo for MIT.
  2. Explainers, diagrams, and math. Manim Community. Motion Canvas for a visual timeline.
  3. Character or cel animation. OpenToonz or Tahoma2D for production 2D, Synfig for rigged vector, Krita or Pencil2D for hand-drawn, Blender Grease Pencil for 2D in 3D.
  4. Motion graphics and compositing. Friction for After Effects-style vector work, Natron for node compositing, Blender’s compositor for 3D-heavy shots.
  5. AI-generated shots and animation. ComfyUI as the workbench with Wan 2.2 (Apache-2.0) first. LTX-2 when you need audio. WanGP on low-VRAM GPUs.
  6. Final assembly. Kdenlive or Shotcut for editing. FFmpeg underneath for encoding and batch jobs.

Read the license gotchas before you ship a product that embeds one of these tools.
