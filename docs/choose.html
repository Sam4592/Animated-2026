How to choose

Match the kind of picture to a path already in this catalog. The table is the short version. The list below is the same choice, by job.

Code-driven, desktop apps, motion graphics, or AI video
You are making Open In this catalog
A video described in code, HTML, or by an agent Code-driven HyperFrames if you think in HTML/CSS. Remotion if you are a React team and fine with its license. Motion Canvas or Revideo for MIT.
Explainers, diagrams, math, encoding, or batch edits Pipeline & CLI Manim Community for math. FFmpeg for encoding and batch jobs. MoviePy for scripted cutting, compositing, captions and titles. editly for a declarative JSON5 spec or CLI to a finished slideshow-style video.
Drawing, rigging, cel work, or a 3D scene 2D/3D apps OpenToonz or Tahoma2D for production 2D. Synfig for rigged vector. Krita or Pencil2D for hand-drawn. Blender Grease Pencil for 2D in 3D.
Keyframed graphics, a node composite, or the final edit Motion graphics Friction for After Effects-style vector work. Natron for node compositing. Blender’s compositor for 3D-heavy shots. Kdenlive or Shotcut for editing.
Generated shots or character animation AI video ComfyUI as the workbench with Wan 2.2 (Apache-2.0) first. LTX-2 when you need audio. WanGP on low-VRAM GPUs.
Vector playback from an After Effects export Lottie & vector lottie-web plays the exported JSON. No video export; render frames through a code-driven pipeline.

By job

  1. Videos from data, templates, or AI agents. HyperFrames if you think in HTML/CSS. Remotion if you are a React team and fine with its license. Motion Canvas or Revideo for MIT.
  2. Explainers, diagrams, and math. Manim Community. Motion Canvas for a visual timeline.
  3. Character or cel animation. OpenToonz or Tahoma2D for production 2D, Synfig for rigged vector, Krita or Pencil2D for hand-drawn, Blender Grease Pencil for 2D in 3D.
  4. Motion graphics and compositing. Friction for After Effects-style vector work, Natron for node compositing, Blender’s compositor for 3D-heavy shots.
  5. AI-generated shots and animation. ComfyUI as the workbench with Wan 2.2 (Apache-2.0) first. LTX-2 when you need audio. WanGP on low-VRAM GPUs.
  6. Final assembly. Kdenlive or Shotcut for editing. FFmpeg underneath for encoding and batch jobs.
